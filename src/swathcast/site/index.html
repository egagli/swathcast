How this map works
  • Map: each selected mission's footprints (Sentinel-1 bursts, Sentinel-2 tiles, Landsat scenes, NISAR frames, MODIS/VIIRS sinusoidal tiles, Sentinel-3 frames; zoomed out, planned swaths), coloured by the time to their next acquisition that passes your filters. Click one for its last 90 days (live from the catalogues) and its plan.
  • AOI: draw or paste one to list its passes: the last 90 days (what each catalogue has now) and the planned ones ahead, each with when it is likely downloadable: acquisition time plus the delivery latency this site measures every hour (Delivery).
  • Sources: plans from ESA, USGS and NASA, timed from the orbits (CelesTrak, SGP4). MODIS, VIIRS and Sentinel-3 have no plans (they image on every orbit): their passes are predicted from the orbit alone, and timed at your AOI. Times are UTC (24 h) and local at the AOI (12 h). Details: README.
