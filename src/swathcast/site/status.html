How this page is kept

Status, dates and notices
Curated from each agency's own announcements (platforms.json in the repository), each with its source; the date each was last reviewed is shown. A satellite marked retired is no longer planned, predicted or projected on the map; its past acquisitions and delivery times stay.
Newest data and check
Updated hourly by this site's poller: the newest sensing time any distributor has delivered for each satellite, per data stream, and whether it is overdue (the satellite check: silent past max(48 h, 1.5× its longest normal gap) beyond its usual delivery time while passes were due). A silent satellite also opens an issue on the repository.
From the agencies' feeds
Fetched daily by the notices job and matched to each satellite by name: loading… A fetched notice that may change a satellite's status (an end of operations, an extension…) opens a review issue on the repository until the curated entry above cites or acknowledges it.
On the map
How far each satellite is planned (the agency's plan) and projected (its last repeat cycle carried forward, in no plan), from the schedule rebuilt daily.
