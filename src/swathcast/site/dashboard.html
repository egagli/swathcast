Definitions and caveats

Tracking universe
Every IW GRDH and IW SLC product ESA's Copernicus Data Space Ecosystem publishes (the _COG duplicates excluded). Other sources are checked for those exact granules by identifier; nothing is sampled.
Available
The first hourly poll that finds the granule at that source. One definition for every source, accurate to about ±1 h. A source faster than CDSE cannot be distinguished from CDSE.
Censoring and timeouts
A granule not yet seen is right-censored at its last check. After the timeout (14 days by default) it counts as not delivered, and "% not delivered" is 1 − F(timeout); it is shown only once some granules have been followed that long.
Partial-coverage sources
Planetary Computer and OPERA do not cover every acquisition. For them, only granules in orbit cells (track × 1-minute along-orbit bin) where the source has delivered at least once are counted.
Excluded rows
Granules first listed long after CDSE published them (the first run, or after an outage) and sightings that followed a poller gap of more than 3 h are excluded from the statistics; the counts are in the summary table's tooltip.
Not measured
Fewer than 20 sightings. No number is ever taken from documentation.
